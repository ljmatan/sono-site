Make it their own

Bigger buttons or more symbols: grid size and tile style

From 2 to 8 columns, with Card or Circle tiles, so every symbol is easy to see and to tap.

Where to find it Caretaker Settings > Board Display

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Sono is a free app for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) on Android: the person taps pictures, and the app speaks for them.

🔲

Grid Size sets how many columns the board shows, from XS with 2 columns to XXXL with 8. Fewer columns mean bigger buttons that are easier to see and to tap. More columns show more symbols at once.

What you can do

  • Seven sizes, from XS (2 columns) to XXXL (8 columns).
  • Display Style switches the tiles between Card and Circle.
  • Change either one at any time without losing symbols, photos or history.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Board Display.
  2. Under Grid Size, pick a size.
  3. Under Display Style, pick Card or Circle.
  4. Go back to the board and give the person time with it before you change it again.

Good to know

  • On first setup Sono picks 3 columns and circles on phones, and 5 columns and cards on large tablets.
  • Fewer columns are a good start for small children, for people with motor difficulties and for anyone who tires quickly.
  • For fewer symbols at a time, combine a small grid with the Menu layout, which shows one category at a time.

Questions and answers

What grid size should a beginner use?

Fewer columns mean bigger buttons, a good start for small children, for people with motor difficulties and for anyone who tires quickly. Sono starts with 3 columns on phones and 5 on large tablets.

Can I make the buttons bigger later?

Yes. Change Grid Size under Caretaker Settings > Board Display at any time. Nothing is lost.
