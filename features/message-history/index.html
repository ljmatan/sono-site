For caretakers

Message history and Recents

See the last 20 sentences spoken, and keep favourite symbols close at hand.

Where to find it Caretaker Settings > Message History, and the Recents tab on the board

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Sono is a free app for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) on Android: the person taps pictures, and the app speaks for them.

🕘

Message History lists the last 20 sentences spoken from the sentence strip, newest first. Reading it in the evening shows which words the person reached for during the day, and gives ideas for what to model next. The Recents tab on the board keeps the last 30 different symbols used, so favourite words stay close.

What you can do

  • The last 20 spoken sentences, newest first.
  • Recents: the last 30 different symbols, newest first, on a tab of their own.
  • Clear History and Clear Recents empty either list.
  • Hidden symbols drop out of Recents.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Message History.
  2. Read the sentences. The Recents symbols themselves are on the board's Recents tab.
  3. To empty a list, tap Clear History or Clear Recents. Sono asks before it clears the history, while Clear Recents empties the Recents tab straight away.

Good to know

  • History and Recents are kept on the device and are not part of Sono's backup file.
  • Recents is a tab in the Advanced and Simple layouts. The Menu layout has no Recents tile.

Questions and answers

Can I see what was said earlier?

Yes. Caretaker Settings > Message History lists the last 20 sentences spoken with Speak, newest first. The list stays on the device.

What is Recents?

A tab on the board with the last 30 different symbols used, so favourite words stay close at hand.
