Talking with symbols

Categories, subcategories and colours

Thirteen categories, each with its own colour, and the option to switch off the ones the person doesn't need.

Where to find it The row of tabs on the board, and Caretaker Settings > Categories

Pobierz z Google Play How to use it

The vocabulary is sorted the way people look for words: People, Basics, Feelings, Actions, Greetings, Manners, Food, Places, Things, Time, Animals, 123s and ABCs. Every category has a colour, and every symbol carries it in its border. Over time the colour itself helps the person find their way: food is always orange, places are always teal, animals are always green.

What you can do

  • Subcategory chips narrow a large category down, such as Family inside People.
  • Switch built-in categories off so the board shows fewer tabs. Restore all brings them back.
  • A category disappears on its own when all of its symbols are hidden.
  • Symbols you make yourself get a word type instead, with colours that follow the Modified Fitzgerald Key, a colour system that groups words by their role in a sentence.
  • Add a category of your own, with a name, an image and a colour.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Categories.
  2. Switch off the categories the person doesn't need yet.
  3. Tap Restore all whenever you want the full board back.

Good to know

  • A category you add yourself starts empty, and symbols can't be moved into it yet. For personal words and photos, use My Symbols.
  • To hide single words instead of whole categories, see hide symbols.
