Make it their own

Record a familiar voice for any symbol

Let a symbol speak in a parent's or a sibling's voice instead of the synthetic one.

Where to find it Caretaker Settings > Customise Symbols, and My Symbols

Disponible en Google Play How to use it
🎙️

A familiar voice can make a word much more inviting, especially for young communicators. Sono lets you record a voice for any symbol. From then on, the recording plays instead of the synthetic voice whenever that symbol is tapped.

What you can do

  • Record a voice for any built-in symbol, and for the symbols you made yourself.
  • Listen back with Play, try again with Retry, and keep it with Save.
  • Remove recording returns the symbol to the synthetic voice.
  • Recordings play offline and stay on the device.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Customise Symbols and tap the symbol.
  2. Tap Record voice, then tap to record and say the word.
  3. Tap Stop recording, then Play to listen.
  4. Tap Save to keep it, or Retry to record it again.

Good to know

  • Record in a quiet room, close to the device, and say just the word.
  • Sono needs microphone access. If it was refused, allow it in the device settings.
  • For a symbol of your own, record the voice while you make it in My Symbols.
  • Recordings are included in backups, so they can move to a new device.
