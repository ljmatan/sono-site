Talking with symbols

Quick phrases for everyday moments

Hi, thank you, help, stop: everyday phrases spoken with one tap, without touching the sentence.

Where to find it The Quick button on the sentence strip, and Caretaker Settings > Quick Phrases

Disponible en Google Play How to use it

Some things need saying right now. The Quick button, the lightning bolt on the sentence strip, opens a row of ready phrases. Each one is spoken the moment it is tapped, and whatever is on the sentence strip stays as it was.

What you can do

  • Sixteen ready phrases: Hi, Bye, Yes, No, Please, Thank you, Sorry, Help, Stop, More, Wait, I love you, I'm fine, I don't know, Excuse me and Come here.
  • Switch off the phrases the person doesn't use, or turn them all back on with Enable All.
  • Add any symbol from the library as a quick phrase with Add from Library.

How to use it

  1. Use the Advanced or Menu layout, where the sentence strip is.
  2. With the strip empty, tap Quick, the lightning bolt.
  3. Tap a phrase. It is spoken straight away.
  4. To change the list, open Caretaker Settings > Quick Phrases.

Good to know

  • On tablets the button carries the word Quick. On phones it shows only the lightning bolt.
  • Your choice of quick phrases is included in backups.
