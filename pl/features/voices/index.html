Voices and languages

Clear speech, with or without internet

Sono speaks with the device's own voice or with an offline neural voice you add, and plays your recordings where you made them.

Where to find it Caretaker Settings > Speech & Voice

Pobierz z Google Play How to use it
🔊

Every tap and every sentence is spoken aloud. By default Sono uses the text-to-speech voice that is already installed on the device for the chosen language. For most languages you can also add an offline neural voice: a natural-sounding voice that runs entirely on the device.

What you can do

  • Fifteen voices across fourteen languages, with English in an American and a British version.
  • Offline neural voices work without internet once they are added, and the text never leaves the device.
  • The voice files are free, published by the open-source Sherpa-onnx project, and Sono points you to the download page.
  • Test plays the voice before you rely on it.
  • A recorded voice on a single symbol plays instead of the synthetic voice.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Speech & Voice and choose the voice for the person's language.
  2. To add an offline neural voice, turn on Allow custom on-device models and confirm.
  3. Tap Improve voice next to the voice.
  4. Add a voice file: download it from a link, add a file you have already downloaded, or tap Open download page to find one.
  5. Sono checks the file and installs it. Tap Test to hear the new voice.

Good to know

  • Voice files are usually 30 to 100 megabytes, so download them over Wi-Fi.
  • Croatian, Bosnian, Serbian and Montenegrin always use the device's own voice.
  • If the device has no voice for the language, Sono says so and uses an English voice until you install one in the device's text-to-speech settings.
  • When the device's own voice is used, the device maker's speech service may send the spoken text to its servers. An offline voice avoids that. The Privacy Policy has the details.
  • You can remove an added voice at any time.
