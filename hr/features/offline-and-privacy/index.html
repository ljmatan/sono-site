Access and privacy

Works offline, stays private

No account, no ads and no tracking: the board, the voice and your photos stay on the device.

Where to find it Everywhere in Sono

Nabavi na Google Play How to use it
🛡️

Sono works from the moment it is installed, without an account. The board, the sentence strip, the games and the drawing board need no internet, and what the person says stays on the device.

What you can do

  • No account, no advertising and no in-app analytics.
  • Messages, custom symbols, photos, recordings and settings are stored on the device.
  • The board and installed offline voices work without internet.
  • Internet is needed only to download lessons and voices, and to open the legal pages.
  • Free to download, with no in-app purchases.

How to use it

  1. Add an offline neural voice under Caretaker Settings > Speech & Voice, so speech doesn't depend on the device maker's speech service.
  2. Download the lessons you want while you are on Wi-Fi. They play offline afterwards.
  3. Export a backup now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe.

Good to know

  • When the device's own voice is used, the device maker's speech service may send the spoken text to its servers.
  • Uninstalling removes the app's data. Exported backups stay where you saved them until you delete them.
  • The Privacy Policy has the full details.
