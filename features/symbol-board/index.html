Talking with symbols

A picture communication board with more than 800 symbols

Picture symbols in thirteen categories, each one spoken aloud the moment it is tapped.

Where to find it The board, the first screen after setup

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Sono is a free app for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) on Android: the person taps pictures, and the app speaks for them.

The board is where the person talks. Every symbol is a simple picture with its word underneath, and a tap says the word straight away. The pictures are sorted into thirteen categories, from People and Feelings to Food, Places and the alphabet, so every word is only a short way away.

What you can do

  • Tap a symbol to hear its word, spoken with the voice chosen during setup.
  • In the Advanced and Simple layouts, move between categories with the row of tabs, or swipe left and right across the symbols. Each category's name is spoken as it opens. In the Menu layout, tap a category tile instead.
  • Narrow a large category down with subcategory chips, such as Family inside People.
  • Every category has its own colour, repeated in the border of each of its symbols, so the person learns where words live. See categories and colours.
  • Add the person's own symbols and photos next to the built-in ones with My Symbols.

How to use it

  1. Open Sono. After the first setup, the board is the first screen you see.
  2. Open a category, for example Food: tap its tab, or its tile in the Menu layout.
  3. Tap a symbol to hear it, for example WATER, then JUICE.
  4. To join words into a message, use the sentence strip.

Good to know

  • Basics and Manners hold many useful first words: YES, NO, HELP, MORE, STOP, ALL DONE, PLEASE and THANK YOU.
  • The board works without internet.
  • If the full board is too much at first, hide symbols or switch categories off, and bring them back later.

Questions and answers

Is there a free picture-symbol communication app for Android?

Yes. Sono is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. It runs on Android phones and tablets and comes with more than 800 picture symbols in thirteen categories.

Does the board need internet?

No. The board works without internet. A connection is needed only to download lessons and voices, and to open the legal pages.
