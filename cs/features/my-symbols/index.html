Make it their own

My Symbols: your own words, pictures and voices

Make symbol cards with your own label, a photo and a recorded voice, for the words no symbol set has.

Where to find it The My Symbols tab on the board, and Caretaker Settings > My Symbols

Získat na Google Play How to use it
⭐

No symbol set has every word a person needs: a cousin's name, a favourite toy, the school bus. With My Symbols you make those cards yourself. The name you give a card becomes its label on the board and the word Sono says, and you can add a photo and a recording of your own.

What you can do

  • Your own label: write the word exactly the way your family says it.
  • A photo from the gallery, or one taken with the camera.
  • A recorded voice that plays instead of the synthetic voice.
  • A word type, such as People, Actions, Things or Feelings, which sets the card's colour.
  • The cards appear on the My Symbols tab, the first tab of the board, and on the My Symbols tile in the Menu layout.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > My Symbols and tap +.
  2. Type a name. This is the label on the card and the word that is spoken.
  3. Pick its Word Type.
  4. Add a photo from the gallery, or take one with the camera.
  5. If you like, tap Record voiceover and say the word.
  6. Tap Create. The new card is on the My Symbols tab straight away.

Good to know

  • Photos of the person's own things, their cup, their dog, their teacher, are often easier to recognise than drawings.
  • Built-in symbols keep their own names. To use a different word, make a card of your own here.
  • In the Advanced layout, the My Symbols tab shows a filter row by word type once your cards span more than one type.
  • Your symbols, photos and recordings stay on the device. Backup and restore moves them to another one.
