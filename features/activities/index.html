Learning and play

Ten short activities for learning and play

Maths, spelling, memory, sorting, listening and more, read aloud and made from the same pictures as the board.

Where to find it The Activities tab on the board, or the Activities tile in the Menu layout

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Sono is a free app for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) on Android: the person taps pictures, and the app speaks for them.

The Activities tab holds ten short games that use the same pictures as the board. Most games read the question aloud with the app's voice, right answers get a small celebration, and most games run for ten rounds: a good length for a short break.

What you can do

  • Math: addition and subtraction, or multiplication and division.
  • Spelling: a picture and its word with one or two letters missing.
  • Odd One Out: four pictures, and one doesn't belong.
  • Memory: match six pairs of symbols.
  • Sorting: which category does this belong to?
  • Flashcard: name the picture from four words.
  • Listening: hear a word and pick the matching picture.
  • Counting: how many are there, from one to five?
  • Comparison: which one is big, hot or fast?
  • Read Aloud: type anything and hear it spoken.

How to use it

  1. On the board, open the Activities tab, the second-to-last tab, or the Activities tile in the Menu layout.
  2. Tap a game. In most games the question is read aloud.
  3. Tap the answer. The home button at the top takes you back to the board.

Good to know

  • There are no difficulty levels, so pick the games that suit the person.
  • Hidden symbols stay out of the games.
  • Use the games as short breaks, not as a replacement for communication.
  • Every activity works offline.

Questions and answers

Are there games to practise symbols with my child?

Yes. The Activities tab has ten short games, including Memory, Sorting, Listening and Flashcard, all made from the board's own pictures.

Do the games work offline?

Yes. Every activity works without internet.
