Make it their own

Hide symbols to start small

Keep the board to the words the person needs today, and bring the rest back with one tap.

Where to find it Caretaker Settings > Hidden Symbols, or Customise Symbols

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Sono is a free app for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) on Android: the person taps pictures, and the app speaks for them.

🙈

A full board can be a lot at first. Hiding symbols keeps the screen calm and the choices clear. Nothing is deleted: hidden symbols wait in settings until you bring them back, one by one or all at once.

What you can do

  • Hidden symbols disappear from the board, from Recents and from the games.
  • Hide a symbol from its sheet in Customise Symbols, or search for it under Hidden Symbols and tap Hide.
  • Restore brings one symbol back, and Restore All brings back every one.
  • A category disappears from the board on its own when all of its symbols are hidden.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Hidden Symbols.
  2. Under Hide a Symbol, search for the word and tap Hide.
  3. To bring a symbol back, tap Restore next to it, or Restore All for every one.

Good to know

  • Because nothing is ever lost, hiding a lot at the start and adding words over time is a good way to begin.
  • To hide whole categories, see categories and colours.
  • Hidden symbols are left out of printed boards too, and the list of hidden symbols is part of backups.

Questions and answers

How do I hide words so a beginner isn't overwhelmed?

Open Caretaker Settings > Hidden Symbols, search for a word and tap Hide. Hidden symbols disappear from the board, from Recents and from the games, and Restore All brings them all back.

Is a hidden symbol deleted?

No. It waits in Hidden Symbols until you restore it.
