Talking with symbols

Build sentences from pictures on the sentence strip

Line up several symbols into one message and say it all with a single tap.

Where to find it The bottom of the board, in the Advanced and Menu layouts

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Sono is a free app for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) on Android: the person taps pictures, and the app speaks for them.

💬

The sentence strip runs along the bottom of the screen. Symbols added to it line up into a message, and Speak says the whole message in one go. A banner shows the sentence for a few seconds, so the person and the listener can both see what was said.

What you can do

  • Double tap a symbol to add it to the strip. A single tap still speaks the word on its own.
  • Prefer one tap? Under Caretaker Settings > Board Display > Tap Behavior, a single tap can add the word instead.
  • Press and hold a symbol and drag it onto the strip.
  • Delete removes the last word, Clear empties the strip, and Speak says the whole sentence.
  • Tap a word on the strip to show the sentence full-screen with a large Speak button. Press and hold a word to remove just that word.
  • The last 20 sentences spoken with Speak on the strip are kept in the message history on the device.

How to use it

  1. Choose the Advanced or Menu layout under Caretaker Settings > Board Display > Board Layout. The Simple layout has no sentence strip.
  2. Double tap the symbols in order, for example I, WANT, JUICE.
  3. Tap Speak. Sono says the sentence, shows it in a banner and clears the strip for the next one.

Good to know

  • Delete, Clear and Speak appear only once there is a word on the strip.
  • In the Menu layout, the category closes back to the menu as soon as a word is added, ready for the next word.
  • A symbol with a recorded voice plays that recording instead of the synthetic voice when it is tapped.
  • Short messages such as WANT + JUICE count too. The strip doesn't ask for perfect grammar.

Questions and answers

How do I build a whole sentence with pictures?

In the Advanced or Menu layout, double tap the symbols in order to add them to the sentence strip, then tap Speak. A single tap only says the word, unless you change Tap Behavior in settings.

Does the sentence have to be grammatically correct?

No. Sono says the words in the order they were added. Short messages such as WANT + JUICE count too.
