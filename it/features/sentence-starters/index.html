Talking with symbols

Sentence starters: “I want…”, “I feel…”

Ready-made sentence beginnings that put the first words on the strip and open the right category.

Where to find it The Start tab on the board, and Caretaker Settings > Sentence Starters

Disponibile su Google Play How to use it
▶️

Many messages begin the same way. The Start tab holds ready-made beginnings such as “I want…”, “I need…”, “I feel…” and “Where is…”. Tapping one puts its words on the sentence strip, says them, and jumps to the category where the sentence usually continues, so a whole sentence takes only a couple of taps.

What you can do

  • Built-in starters for everyday messages, including “I want…”, “I need…”, “I feel…”, “I like…”, “Where is…”, “Can I…” and “It hurts…”.
  • Switch off the starters the person doesn't need, so the Start tab stays short.
  • Write your own starters with their own words, picture and colour, for example a phrase your family uses every day.

How to use it

  1. Open Caretaker Settings > Sentence Starters.
  2. Switch starters on or off. Only the ones switched on appear on the Start tab.
  3. To write your own, tap Add starter, type the phrase, choose an image and a colour, and tap Create.
  4. On the board, open the Start tab and tap a starter. Add the rest of the sentence, then tap Speak.

Good to know

  • The Start tab is part of the Advanced and Simple layouts. It works best in Advanced, where the rest of the sentence can be added on the sentence strip. The Menu layout has no Start tile.
  • Your starters are included in backups.
