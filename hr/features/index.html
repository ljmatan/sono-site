Everything Sono can do

Every feature has its own page: what it does, where to find it in the app, and how to use it, step by step.

Talking with symbols

The board, the sentence strip and the shortcuts that make a message quick.

  • 🖼️ A board of more than 800 picture symbols Hand-drawn picture symbols in thirteen categories, each one spoken aloud the moment it is tapped. Learn more →
  • 💬 Build sentences on the sentence strip Line up several symbols into one message and say it all with a single tap. Learn more →
  • ▶️ Sentence starters: “I want…”, “I feel…” Ready-made sentence beginnings that put the first words on the strip and open the right category. Learn more →
  • ⚡ Quick phrases for everyday moments Hi, thank you, help, stop: everyday phrases spoken with one tap, without touching the sentence. Learn more →
  • 🔍 Search for any symbol Type part of a word and the matching symbols appear, ready to add to the sentence. Learn more →
  • ✏️ A drawing board for what words can't say Sketch a quick picture with six colours, three pen sizes, an eraser and undo. Learn more →
  • 🗂️ Categories, subcategories and colours Thirteen categories, each with its own colour, and the option to switch off the ones the person doesn't need. Learn more →

Make it their own

Photos, voices, words and layouts that fit one particular person.

  • 📐 Three board layouts: Simple, Advanced and Menu Choose how much the screen shows, from one tap for one word to full sentence building, and try each layout before you decide. Learn more →
  • 🔲 Bigger buttons or more symbols: grid size and tile style From 2 to 8 columns, with round or square tiles, so every symbol is easy to see and to tap. Learn more →
  • ⭐ My Symbols: your own words, pictures and voices Make symbol cards with your own label, a photo and a recorded voice, for the words no symbol set has. Learn more →
  • 📷 Put your own photo on any symbol Replace any built-in picture with a photo of the real thing: their cup, their dog, their grandmother. Learn more →
  • 🎙️ Record a familiar voice for any symbol Let a symbol speak in a parent's or a sibling's voice instead of the synthetic one. Learn more →
  • 🙈 Hide symbols to start small Keep the board to the words the person needs today, and bring the rest back with one tap. Learn more →
  • 🎨 Colours, appearance and picture style Choose the accent colour, a light or dark look, uppercase text, and how the people in the pictures look. Learn more →

Voices and languages

How Sono speaks, and in which languages.

  • 🔊 Clear speech, with or without internet Sono speaks with the device's own voice or with an offline neural voice you add, and plays your recordings where you made them. Learn more →
  • 🌍 Fourteen languages that switch together The screens, the symbol names and the voice change language together, with one setting. Learn more →

Learning and play

Games and narrated lessons made from the same pictures as the board.

  • 🎮 Ten short activities for learning and play Maths, spelling, memory, sorting, listening and more, read aloud and made from the same pictures as the board. Learn more →
  • 📚 Narrated picture lessons Short illustrated stories that turn everyday words into little films, read aloud with each word highlighted. Learn more →

For caretakers

Tools that keep the board safe, backed up and on paper.

  • 🔒 Caretaker lock Settings stay behind a short sum, so the board can't be changed by an accidental tap. Learn more →
  • 📌 Kiosk mode: keep the device on the board Keep the screen awake, show Sono on the lock screen and pin the app, so the board is always ready. Learn more →
  • 🕘 Message history and Recents See the last 20 sentences spoken, and keep favourite symbols close at hand. Learn more →
  • 🖨️ Print boards as PDF Turn any category, or the whole board, into an A4 PDF to print or share: a paper board for the bath, the pool or a flat battery. Learn more →
  • 💾 Backup and restore Save your custom symbols, photos, recordings and settings in one file, and move them to another device. Learn more →

Access and privacy

Ways in for different abilities, and what stays on the device.

  • ♿ Touch, keyboard and screen reader Large tap targets, colour cues, full keyboard control and screen reader support, with honest notes on what isn't there yet. Learn more →
  • 🛡️ Works offline, stays private No account, no ads and no tracking: the board, the voice and your photos stay on the device. Learn more →
